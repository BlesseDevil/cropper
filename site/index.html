    How to use SliceDevil

    1. Load a photo. Drop it anywhere, paste with Ctrl+V, click the circle, or press Open.
    2. Shape the crop. Choose Rectangle or Ellipse, a ratio, and Centred or Free drag. Pan and zoom the photo behind the box.
    3. Straighten it. Open Rotate and turn the photo or the box, one at a time. Keys: [ and ].
    4. For a government form? Once a photo is loaded, press Govt. form, then Photo (300×300, up to 100 KB) or Signature (300×80, up to 60 KB). Export tunes the JPEG size for you.
    5. Export. Pick a size and format, then Export. Nothing leaves your device.
